Questions parents ask

Do we both need to pay?

No. One plan per family covers both parents and all the children.

Does my co-parent need the app too?

Yes. Each parent uses their own account on their own phone. You invite them with a simple code. Most features, such as agreeing to a schedule, need both of you.

Is KinNest a court-approved record or legal advice?

No. KinNest keeps a clear record of what you both agreed, with dates and history. It isn't a court order and it isn't legal advice. If you need an agreement approved by a court, ask a lawyer or mediator.

Can messages or agreements be edited or deleted?

Messages can't be edited or deleted. Agreed schedules, plan items and agreed times can't be changed quietly either. To change something, you send a new proposal and your co-parent agrees to it.

What happens if we stop paying?

Your family's records stay readable and can be exported. We send reminders well before anything is removed, and records are removed only after about a year without a plan.

Can I download my records?

Yes. Either parent can export messages, expenses, the parenting plan and more as a PDF or spreadsheet.

Who can see our information?

Only the two parents in your family. Each family is its own separate space. Please read our Privacy Policy for the details.

Which phones does it work on?

Android now, and iPhone is coming soon.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account in the app at any time, or follow the steps on our delete account page.
