Privacy Policy

DRAFT FOR LAWYER REVIEW. NOT YET LEGAL ADVICE OR FINAL. Last updated: October 22, 2026.

Who we are. KinNest is provided by IPHENNA LLC (“iphenna”, “we”, “us”). Contact for privacy requests: privacy@iphenna.com. General questions: kinnest@iphenna.com.

What KinNest is. A shared app for two parents who live apart to keep a calendar, messages, expenses and handoff notes in one place.

1. What we collect

You give us:

  • Your email address and password (the password is stored only in scrambled form by our sign-in provider).
  • Your full name and the display name you choose.
  • Your children's first names (only first names; please do not enter more than you need to).
  • Custody schedule, calendar events, swap requests.
  • Parenting plan: agreed items by topic, up to 400 characters each, proposals, answers, on/off state and history.
  • Messages you send to your co-parent.
  • Expenses: amounts, categories, descriptions, dates, notes, standing bills, replies on expenses, and receipt photos.
  • Handoff notes. These can include health information about a child (for example medication or sleep). Only enter what you are comfortable sharing with your co-parent.

Created by the app:

  • Notification settings and a device token for each phone you sign in on (so we can send notifications).
  • Records that you accepted our Terms and this Policy (which version, when).
  • If you request account deletion: the request, its dates, and the name you confirmed it with.
  • A record of changes to your name.
  • A record made when you export a document: an export ID, a fingerprint (hash) of the exported content, the date range, the sections included, your full name and the time, so a copy can be checked later. The record is visible only to the exporter and is not shown to the other parent.

What we do not collect: We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking tools, contacts, or access to your photo library (receipt photos are only those you take or choose). We do not use GPS or your device's location. You can type place names (for example a school) into events and handoff notes yourself. Receipt text is read on your phone and is not sent to us for reading.

2. How we use it

Only to run the app: to show your co-parent the shared information, to send notifications, to keep the permanent record you and your co-parent rely on, to handle account deletion, billing, security and support, and to meet legal duties. We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not use your messages or notes to train AI models.

Notifications say only that something happened (for example "Sam sent you a message"), never the content.

3. Who can see it

  • Your co-parent sees everything in your shared family space. Nobody else in the app can.
  • Our service providers process data for us: Supabase (data storage and sign-in); Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (delivers push notifications; receives a device token and generic notification text); RevenueCat (subscriptions); Google Play and Apple App Store (payments). Google Play or the Apple App Store processes payments and we never see card details. We do not use advertising or analytics services and do not sell data.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it or to protect safety, after we check the request is valid.

4. Messages and records are permanent

Messages cannot be edited or deleted by either parent. This protects both of you. A calendar event you created can be deleted within 1 hour (after that it can only be marked cancelled), the same way handoff notes can. Handoff notes can be edited for 24 hours and deleted for 1 hour; expenses follow the approval rules in the app.

5. Deleting your account

  • You can ask to delete your account in Settings. Your co-parent is told in the app and by notification.
  • If you share a family with a co-parent, deletion happens after 30 days unless you cancel. If you are alone in your family, it happens within about 10 minutes.
  • When your account is deleted we remove your sign-in, email, password and notification tokens. We keep a normalized version of the email address and the date of the first free trial, only to stop repeated free trials and fraud. Your full name stays on messages, expenses and notes you created, marked "(former member)", because your co-parent's records need to show who wrote them.
  • Your co-parent keeps a read-only archive of the family records for 12 months, and can export it at any time.
  • If both parents delete their accounts, the whole family space, including receipt photos, is deleted.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel any subscription you bought through Apple or Google. Cancel it in your store account.
  • Backups may keep deleted data for up to 30 days before they are overwritten.

6. How long we keep things

While your family space is active: until you or your co-parent change or delete the item where the app allows it. After deletion: see section 5. Legal records of consent and deletion requests are kept as required by law.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to see, correct, export or delete your data, to object to some uses, and to complain to a regulator. You can see and export your data in the app; for anything else write to privacy@iphenna.com. We reply within 30 days. Where the data is a shared record with your co-parent, we may keep what the other parent needs, as described above.

8. Children

KinNest is for adults (18 or older). Children do not use it. Parents enter children's first names and, in notes, may enter health details. We use these only to provide the app to the parents. If you believe a child has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our hosting provider. Each family's data is separated by database rules so only its two parents can read it. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at privacy@iphenna.com if you find a problem.

10. Transfers

Our service providers process data in the United States under standard data protection terms.

11. Changes

If we change this Policy in a meaningful way we will ask you to accept it again in the app. The version date is at the top.

12. Contact

IPHENNA LLC. Privacy requests: privacy@iphenna.com. General questions: kinnest@iphenna.com.
